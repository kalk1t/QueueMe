# Release 1 Out-of-Scope

## 1) Explicitly prohibited in Release 1

The following are not part of Release 1:

- Medical clinic workflows
- Restaurant/table-management workflows
- Appointments
- Appointment-booking models
- Customer mobile application
- iOS business application
- Marketing messages
- Marketing message channels
- Public display screen modes except approved pilot requirements
- Kiosk mode except approved pilot requirements
- Full two-way staff/customer chat
- Full two-way messaging/chat
- POS integrations
- Calendar integrations
- Public API
- White labeling
- Loyalty, reviews, and reward point systems
- AI wait prediction
- Voice notifications
- Voice notification channels
- WhatsApp channel support
- File-upload custom questions
- File upload custom questions
- More than five custom questions
- More than five active custom questions
- Priority or VIP queue rules
- Multi-device offline mutations

## 2) Deferred product capabilities

Deferred to later milestones unless explicitly added in a new scope decision:

- Multi-channel outreach beyond SMS
- Deep integrations with scheduling tools, POS providers, and CRM systems
- Marketing campaign modules
- Staff-administered queue transfer workflow across independent operators
- Formal white-label tenant branding
- Voice and WhatsApp communications

## 3) Potential pilot-triggered additions

The following may be considered only if an approved pilot contract explicitly requires them and adds a new milestone decision:

- Kiosk mode
- Public waiting-room display

No other deferred capability may be implemented without an updated milestone and explicit scope change.

## 4) Future regulated-industry considerations

Release 1 is not designed for regulated clinical, financial, or medical workflows. If later roadmap expansions target regulated industries:

- Consent models must be revalidated by legal and compliance
- Retention and deletion policies must be re-audited
- Notification policy may need additional controls

## 5) Conditions required to reconsider a deferred item

Reconsideration is only possible when all are true:

1. User impact and compliance implications are documented
2. Product owner and platform administrator approve an explicit scope change
3. Corresponding roadmap milestone and budget are approved
4. Security and privacy implications are represented in a milestone-specific PRD
5. Tenant-isolation and billing behavior is not adversely affected
